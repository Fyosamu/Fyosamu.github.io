Questions people actually ask

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

No, and neither can anyone else — rankings depend on competition, authority and changes Google makes without telling us. We guarantee a written audit, the fixes applied, and before/after numbers so you can judge the work on evidence.

How long before I see a difference?

It depends on how often the site is crawled and how competitive the queries are. Fixes that remove an indexing block can show up within days; changes to content structure take longer. We re-run the crawl at handover so there is a dated number either way.

Do I need to rebuild my website?

Almost never. Most technical problems live in templates, headers and configuration, which can be fixed in place. If something genuinely needs a rebuild, we will say so instead of inventing work.

What access do you need?

The URL to start. Search Console read-only access makes the audit far more accurate, and applying the fixes needs whatever controls the templates — CMS admin, repository access, or your developer for the parts we cannot reach.

Is my site too small to bother?

Small sites usually have fewer problems and benefit more from each fix, because there are fewer pages competing with each other. If we look at it and decide it is not worth $300, we will tell you that instead of selling it.

Why do you only take crypto?

We do not have access to bank or card processors where we are. USDT on BEP-20 or ERC-20, or escrow on LaborX if you would rather not send funds first. It also means no invoice delays and no chargebacks.
