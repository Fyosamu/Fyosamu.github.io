Where the time usually goes

Slow sites are rarely slow for a mysterious reason. These four account for most of what we find.

Bytes

Unoptimized media

PNGs where WebP or AVIF belongs, hero images the size of a phone screen served at four times that, no lazy loading, and video that autoplays above the fold.

Render

Blocking CSS and JS

Stylesheets and scripts the page cannot paint without, third-party tags loading in the critical path, and a bundle shipping libraries that are used once.

Server

Slow response

Uncached pages generated from scratch on every request, no CDN in front of static assets, and database work repeated because a query was never indexed.

Measure

Core Web Vitals, not vibes

LCP, INP and CLS are the numbers Google actually uses. We read them from field data where it exists — real visitors, real devices — rather than a lab test on a fast desktop connection.

What you get

  • A Lighthouse run before and after — two dated reports, so the improvement is a fact, not a claim
  • Images rebuilt properly — modern formats, responsive sizes, lazy loading below the fold
  • Critical CSS inlined, the rest deferred — the page paints without waiting for a stylesheet
  • Bundle trimmed — unused code removed, heavy libraries replaced or loaded only where needed
  • Caching and CDN configured — repeat visits and static assets stop hitting the origin
  • Nothing added that you now have to maintain — no new framework, no proprietary plugin
Honest disclaimer: we do not promise a specific Lighthouse score, because the final number depends on your hosting and on third-party tags we do not control. We promise the measured before/after and the work that produced it.

Questions people actually ask

Will you guarantee a Lighthouse score of 90 or more?

No. The score depends on your hosting and on third-party tags such as chat widgets, analytics and ad scripts. What we do is fix everything under our control and show you the measured before and after, so you can see exactly what moved and what still blocks it.

Could optimisation break my site?

Changes are made in a way you can review and revert, and the site is checked after each round. Anything that would alter how the site looks or behaves gets flagged first rather than quietly shipped.

What access do you need?

The URL for the audit. Applying the fixes needs the source — a repository, or the CMS if the site is built on one. If your developer would rather do it, we can hand over a prioritised list instead, though the price stays the same.

Is 1 to 3 days realistic for a large site?

For the shared templates, yes — and on most sites the templates are where the gain is. If your project has hundreds of unique page types, we will scope it honestly rather than pretend the flat rate covers it.

Do you offer website care and SEO as well?

Yes. Speed work and technical SEO touch the same templates, so they combine well, and the care plan keeps the site fast after we leave.

Why do you only take crypto?
