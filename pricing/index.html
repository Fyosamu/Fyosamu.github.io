Straight answers

Why are the prices fixed and published?

Because the usual agency dance — contact form, discovery call, forty-five minutes, then a number — wastes everyone's afternoon and usually ends in a quote nobody compares. Publishing the list filters out the people who were never going to buy and gets the ones who were straight to a decision.

Do I have to book a call to see a price?

No. That is the entire point of this page. If the price fits, email us; if it doesn't, you found out in thirty seconds instead of an hour.

How do I place an order?

You email hkay7645@gmail.com — type it into your own mail app, or use any "order →" link on this page. Send what you need and when you need it, and you get a fixed price and a delivery date back. There is no checkout, no account, and no payment taken before you have agreed the number.

Why is payment in USDT?

Because cards and PayPal are unreliable or simply unavailable in the countries many of our clients work from. We take BEP-20 or ERC-20 only — never TRC-20, because that address does not exist on Tron and the transfer would be lost.

What if my project is bigger than these packages?

Email the scope anyway. Larger work is quoted the same way: a fixed number agreed up front, broken into stages if that is easier. The price list is a starting point, not a ceiling.

What if I want someone else to send you the client?

The partner program pays 15% of the fee on anything you send our way, with no cap and no exclusivity. It is described on the partners page.
