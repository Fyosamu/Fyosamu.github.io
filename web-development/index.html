Questions people actually ask

Theme or custom — which should I pick?

If the site's job is to look credible and take enquiries, a theme is usually right and saves you money. If the site's job is to sell something unusual, or the brand can't survive a template, go custom. We'll say which one honestly after seeing your brief.

How long does a website take?

3 to 7 days for a theme-based site or landing page; longer for custom builds and dashboards. The deadline is fixed in writing before we start.

Do I own the website afterwards?

Yes. You get the full source, the database and the deployment instructions. Nothing is held hostage and there is no licence fee payable to us.

Can you work with my designer?

Yes. Send the files and we build to them. If you don't have a designer, we handle layout and typography as part of the build.

Why do you only take crypto?

We don't have access to bank or card processors where we are. USDT on BEP-20 or ERC-20, or escrow on LaborX if you'd rather not send funds first.
