Questions people actually ask

Do I need to change my website first?

Usually not. We audit it first and tell you honestly if something will look broken inside an app shell. Small fixes are quoted separately so nothing is hidden.

Will the app work if my site goes down?

The shell and cached content stay on the phone, so the app opens and shows what it has. Live data needs the site — the same dependency you already have.

How much does it cost to publish on the App Store and Play Store?

The stores charge their own fees (Google Play is a one-time $25; Apple is $99/year). Those are paid by you, directly to Google and Apple — we never touch them.

Can I publish it under my own developer account?

Yes, and most clients do. We can hand you a signed build and the documentation, or submit it through your account if you give us access.

Why do you only take crypto?
