Questions people actually ask

Do we have to use n8n?

No. Visual builders are right when the flow is linear and the connectors exist. When you hit rate limits, branching, custom auth or a retry the builder can't express, we drop to Python or Node. You own the instance and the workflow files either way.

We have Zaps that keep breaking. Can you fix them?

Send us the failing one. Most breakages are expired auth, pagination the trigger never handled, or a rate limit with no backoff. We fix the root cause and add retries plus alerting, so it fails loudly instead of quietly at 3am.

Can you work with our AI provider?

Yes — OpenAI, Anthropic, an open-weights model you host yourself, or whatever key you already have. Prompts, models and thresholds stay in config, so you can swap providers without a rewrite.

Do you need access to our systems?

Least privilege: a service account with the narrowest scope that works, secrets kept in your vault, and the workflow definitions handed back to you at the end. You can revoke everything the day we finish.

How do you price an automation?

Fixed quote after one message describing the workflow. If it turns out to be two projects, we say so and quote them separately rather than burying it in a number.

Why do you only take crypto?

We don't have access to bank or card processors where we are. USDT on BEP-20 or ERC-20, or escrow on LaborX. No invoices with a company name, no chargebacks.
