What it does

Post scheduling

Absolute times, relative delays and recurring schedules — all timezone-correct. Queue a week of posts in one sitting and let it publish.

RSS / Atom auto-posting

Point it at any feed. New items get published to your channel automatically, with a baseline on first poll so old articles never flood it.

Rate-limit aware

Telegram rate limits are retried, not dropped. A network hiccup leaves the post pending instead of losing it.
