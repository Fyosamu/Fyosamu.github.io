The terms, in plain words

Everything below goes into a short written agreement before the first project, so nobody argues later.

What you get

  • 15% of every payment we receive from the project you introduced
  • Paid in USDT — BEP-20 (BNB Chain) or ERC-20 (Ethereum / Base / Polygon), to your wallet
  • Within 24 hours of each payment clearing, not at the end of some quarter
  • On-chain visibility — you can verify every payment yourself
  • Indefinite — one project or a steady flow, both fine

What you don't get stuck with

  • No joining fee, no subscription, no minimum quota
  • No exclusivity — you can work with anyone else too
  • No selling: we write the proposal, the quote and the contract
  • No delivery risk — the build quality is ours, not yours
  • No cold calling — we only ever contact a client you introduced
How we calculate it: 15% of the amount we actually receive on the project you introduced — not of a quote that was never paid. If the client only pays in stages, you get 15% of each stage as it clears.
Not TRC-20. Our wallets are EVM addresses. USDT must be sent on BEP-20 or ERC-20 — that address does not exist on Tron, and a TRC-20 transfer to it would be lost.

Straight answers

What if the client doesn't close?

Then there's nothing to pay and nothing owed. You only earn when we get paid, and we only get paid when the project ships and the client pays.

Can I put my own price on top?

Yes. If you're selling the project as your own service, set whatever margin you like and invoice the client yourself. Our 15% is calculated on what we are paid by you.

What kind of projects can I send?

Websites (theme or custom), turning an existing site into an Android/iOS app, Telegram/Discord/WhatsApp bots, technical SEO, performance work and site security. If it's outside that, ask anyway — we'll say no quickly if it is.

Will you contact my client directly?

Not unless you ask us to. By default we talk to you, you talk to them. If you'd rather we run the account directly, we can — under a white-label arrangement.

Why crypto only?

We're paid in USDT because we don't have access to bank or card processors in our jurisdiction. It also means no invoice delays and no chargebacks — the money you see clear is the money that's final.

How do I know you'll actually pay?

You don't have to take our word for it: we'll put the 15% and the 24-hour window in writing before the first project, and every payment we receive from your introduction is on-chain and checkable. Start with one project.
